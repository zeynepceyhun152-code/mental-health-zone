Questions

How much time does it take?

It's up to you. You only need to run one activity a year, and bringing it to your school is easy. A short activity can take less than 15 minutes a week. If you want to put in more effort, it can be about an hour a week. It depends on how much you want to impact your community.

Do I need experience?

No. You don't need any experience.

Can my existing club join?

Yes! We highly encourage it.

Is it free?

It's completely free. You don't need to pay anything. We're a nonprofit-style student project.
